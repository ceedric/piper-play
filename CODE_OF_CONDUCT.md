# How we treat each other

This project is for artists, especially Indigenous and neurodivergent artists, and anyone who has felt that technology wasn't made for them.

**Here, we:**

- Assume every question is a good one. Nobody is "too beginner."
- Explain things in plain language, and are patient when we need to explain again.
- Respect that people learn, read, and communicate in different ways and at different speeds.
- Respect cultural knowledge. Words, stories, and voices from a community belong to that community. Don't share or ask for what isn't yours to share.
- Give credit to the people whose work we build on.

**We don't accept:**

- Mocking, talking down, or "just Google it" answers.
- Racism, ableism, transphobia, sexism, or any harassment.
- Using this tool to imitate a real person's voice or words without their consent.

If someone makes this space feel unsafe, contact the project owner through GitHub. People who don't follow these guidelines may be removed from the project.
